# ABYSS V11.2 — structural loading update

- Reduced nominal yield moment/shear resistance slightly across ships. Source is gameplay calibration, not a certified ship material dataset.
- Slightly increased visible bending under uneven buoyancy, within restrained deflection/slope limits.
- Edmund Fitzgerald has a somewhat lower local structural strength and faster cut-tip fatigue, with mild additional material damage only under a large uneven tsunami load.
- Qualified large cutter slits progress under the remaining section’s weight more readily. Small holes still have no structural fracture permission.
- Extreme-wave damage creates at most one small unseeded notch per tsunami on Edmund Fitzgerald; no cascading tsunami-driven disintegration.
- All flooding, rigging, lights, telegraph, controls, and PWA support retained from 11.1.
