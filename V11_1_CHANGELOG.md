# ABYSS V11.1 — Faster flooding and stronger sinking response

- Increased effective inlet area of hull breaches so submerged tears and ruptures flood substantially faster than V11.0.
- Increased flow capacity through damaged bulkhead openings and open watertight doors, while retaining pressure-head limits and compartment capacity conservation.
- Increased submerged funnel uptake flooding rate.
- Increased the effective flooding load in the 2D buoyancy model by 38% to compensate for unresolved reserve-buoyancy loss in the simplified side-view model. This is **not** a real-world change to water density and has not been calibrated against full-scale stability plans.
- Preserves compartment-specific permeability, pressure difference, keel-hinge physics, rigging, rotating lights and all ship textures.

This model is not historically validated for every vessel. The Titanic four-forward-compartment benchmark remains a target for further simulation calibration.
